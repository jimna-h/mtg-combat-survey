Magic: The Gathering Combat Survey

This is a short, voluntary class-project survey. You'll work through 10 combat scenarios from a card game. No names are collected and your answers are used only for this project. You can stop at any time. If you choose to share an email below, it is stored separately from your answers and used only to send you the results.

You don't need to know the rules in advance, and we don't expect you to know the answers. Please do your best to figure out what happens in each combat scenario based on their abilities.
